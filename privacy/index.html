Privacy Policy

How ES Designs handles your data in Thrustline, our other Meta Quest apps, and on this website.

Last Updated: October 8, 2026

1. Introduction

ES Designs (“we,” “us,” or “our”) is an independent studio that builds applications for Meta Quest and operates the website esdesigns.org. This privacy policy explains how each of our applications, and this website, handles your data.

This policy applies to:

  • Thrustline: a VR racing game for Meta Quest, powered by your workout on a cardio machine
  • Vantura: a 360 video platform for higher education and CTE, made up of a Quest app and an instructor web portal
  • Anatomy & Physiology Lab: an immersive VR educational experience for Meta Quest
  • esdesigns.org: this website, including its contact forms and email signups

These differ significantly in how they handle data. Thrustline and the Anatomy & Physiology Lab need no account and send nothing to us; what Thrustline saves stays on your headset. Vantura is an account-based service that a school uses to deliver its own video to its own students, so it necessarily stores account and usage information. This website uses analytics and stores what you send through its forms. Each is described separately below. We never sell personal information, and we never use it for advertising.

2. Thrustline

Thrustline collects no personal information and sends nothing to ES Designs. It has no account, login, or registration, never asks for your name, email address, age, or weight, contains no advertising, and uses no analytics service. Its own code makes no network requests.

What Thrustline saves on your headset

To keep your progress between sessions, Thrustline saves the following in its private storage on your Meta Quest:

  • Your settings: music, volume, comfort vignette, and whether ghosts are shown
  • Your coins, the bikes and paint finishes you have bought with them, and your progress against the in-game rivals
  • Machine profiles: the movement pattern of your machine (for example the rhythm and size of an elliptical stride or a rowing stroke), measured during calibration so the game can read your effort
  • Race records: the date and time, course, difficulty, machine profile, lap times, coins collected, distance, speed, effort levels, time spent flying, and estimated calories for each race
  • Ghost lines: the path your bike took in a race, saved ten times a second as a position and direction, so you can race against your own best runs
  • Lifetime totals, such as distance and estimated calories

This data is stored only on your headset. It is never transmitted to us or to any third party, and Thrustline excludes it from Android device backups.

Estimated calories

Calorie figures are an estimate calculated from your effort relative to your own calibrated pace. Thrustline does not ask for your weight, age, sex, or heart rate. It is a game, not a medical or fitness measurement device.

Rivals

The named rivals you race against are recordings of laps ridden by the developer, included in the app. Thrustline does not download, share, or use any other player’s data.

Deleting your data

In Thrustline, Settings › Reset deletes your race records, ghost lines, stats, and course progress; it keeps your coins, bikes, machine profiles, and settings. Uninstalling Thrustline removes everything it has saved. Because we never receive any of this data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Purchases

Thrustline is sold through the Meta Horizon Store. Meta processes the payment and manages your purchase. The app may ask Meta’s platform to confirm that your account owns Thrustline; that check takes place between the app and Meta, and we do not receive your Meta account details or payment information.

3. Vantura

Vantura lets faculty upload 360 videos they have filmed and deliver them to Quest headsets their institution already owns. It requires an account, and it communicates with our servers. This section describes exactly what that involves.

Account information

Signing in to the Vantura portal or the Quest app requires an ES Designs account. We store your email address, an account identifier, and your role (student, instructor, or administrator). Authentication is handled by Supabase. We do not store your password; it is held by our authentication provider in hashed form and is never visible to us.

Classes and enrollment

Instructors create classes, and students join them with a short join code. We store which classes exist, who teaches them, who is enrolled, and when they enrolled. This is what determines which videos a given headset is allowed to download.

Headset information

When a headset is paired to an account, the Vantura app reports information about that headset so an instructor can confirm a cart is ready before class:

  • A device identifier generated by the app
  • The headset model (for example, “Oculus Quest 3”)
  • The installed app version
  • Available free storage space on the headset
  • Which lesson videos are currently downloaded, their size, and when they downloaded

We do not collect headset serial numbers, advertising identifiers, or any Meta account information.

Viewing activity

When a video is watched in the Vantura app, we record a session containing which video was watched, the class it belongs to, when viewing started and ended, how long it was actually playing, the headset model, and the app version. Time spent paused is excluded from the recorded duration.

This activity is tied to the signed-in account, and an instructor can see it for their own class. It exists so faculty can tell whether students completed an assigned lesson. It is not used for profiling, advertising, or any purpose beyond reporting to the instructor and the institution.

Video content

Videos uploaded by faculty are stored in Cloudflare R2 object storage, along with a title, description, and technical details such as duration, resolution, and codec. Access is granted through short-lived signed links issued only to accounts enrolled in the relevant class. The institution owns this content; we host and deliver it on the institution’s behalf.

Stored on the headset

Vantura is download-first, so lessons play without a network connection. Downloaded video files and a copy of the class lesson list are stored in the app’s private storage on the headset, along with the sign-in tokens that keep you logged in. Signing out or uninstalling the app removes them from the device.

4. Anatomy & Physiology Lab

The Anatomy & Physiology Lab collects no personal information. It requires no account, login, registration, or email address, makes no network requests, and does not communicate with any external server, API, or third-party service.

It keeps quiz progress in session memory only. Nothing is written to your device’s storage, and when you close the app, all session data is cleared automatically.

5. This Website (esdesigns.org)

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use this site: which pages are viewed, which buttons and links are clicked, the referring site, the type of device and browser, and an approximate location (country or city) derived from your IP address. Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser to do this. We use these reports only to improve the site. You can block these cookies in your browser settings, or install Google’s Analytics opt-out add-on.

Contact forms

When you send a message through a form on this site, we receive what you type (usually your name, email address, and message, and your institution where a form asks for it) along with which form you used. Submissions pass through a Cloudflare Turnstile check that screens out automated spam, and are stored in our Supabase database, which notifies us by email. We use them only to reply to you.

Email signups

If you join the Thrustline launch list, we store your email address and use it to tell you when Thrustline is available and to send occasional build notes from ES Designs. Newsletter subscriptions are managed through Beehiiv, our email provider. Every email includes an unsubscribe link, and you can also ask us to remove you at any time.

Hosting and fonts

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. Its fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when your browser requests them.

6. Information We Do Not Collect

For full transparency, none of our Meta Quest applications collect any of the following:

  • Location or GPS data
  • Financial or payment information (purchases on the Meta Horizon Store are handled by Meta, and Vantura billing is handled by our payment processor; neither reaches our servers)
  • Advertising identifiers, or any data used for advertising
  • Social media data
  • Microphone or camera recordings
  • Eye, face, or body tracking data, or any other biometric data
  • Scans or maps of your room
  • Contacts, calendars, or files outside the application

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

7. Device Permissions

Our apps use certain VR hardware features solely to make the app work, not for data collection. Sensor data is processed on the headset in real time and is never transmitted to us.

Thrustline
  • Head tracking: reads the rhythm of your movement on your machine to set your speed and steering. It is processed in real time; only the results described in Section 2 (such as your race path) are saved, and only on your headset.
  • Hand tracking: lets you use the menus with your hands instead of controllers. Processed in real time; hand data is never recorded or transmitted.
  • Passthrough: shows your real surroundings while you set up and calibrate. The headset’s system draws the passthrough view; Thrustline never receives camera images.
Vantura
  • Head tracking: required to display 360 video, so the view follows where you look. Processed in real time.
  • Hand tracking: lets you operate the menus with your hands instead of controllers. Processed in real time; hand data is never recorded or transmitted.
  • Network access: used to sign in, download assigned lesson videos, and report class readiness and viewing activity as described in Section 3.
Anatomy & Physiology Lab
  • Hand tracking: enables natural hand interactions with 3D anatomy models and menus. Processed in real time.
  • Eye tracking: used for gaze-based interaction with educational content. Processed in real time; gaze data is never recorded or transmitted.
  • Passthrough: blends the real-world environment with virtual content. The headset’s system draws the passthrough view, and video never leaves the device.

8. Student Data and Institutional Use

When an institution uses Vantura with enrolled students, the account, enrollment, and viewing records described in Section 3 may constitute education records under the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA) and comparable state laws.

The institution controls those records. We process them only to provide the service to that institution, under its direction. We do not use student data to build advertising profiles, to train machine learning models, or for any purpose other than operating Vantura for the school. An institution may request export or deletion of its data at any time, and we will act on that request.

Institutions that require a written data protection agreement or student data privacy addendum should contact us using the details below.

9. Third-Party Services

Thrustline and the Anatomy & Physiology Lab use no third-party services of their own.

Vantura relies on the following providers to operate:

  • Supabase: authentication, database, and application logic
  • Cloudflare R2: storage and delivery of uploaded video files
  • Cloudflare Turnstile: bot protection on portal sign-in
  • Vercel: hosting for the instructor web portal

This website relies on:

  • Google Analytics: site usage reports
  • Supabase: storage of form submissions and email signups
  • Vercel and Cloudflare Turnstile: receiving form submissions and screening out spam
  • Resend: the email notification we receive when a form is submitted
  • FormSubmit: a backup email delivery path for forms, used only if our database cannot be reached
  • Beehiiv: newsletter delivery and subscription management
  • GitHub Pages and Google Fonts: hosting and fonts

These providers process data on our behalf in order to run our services. They are not permitted to use it for their own purposes, except as described in their own privacy policies (for example, Google’s handling of Analytics data).

Our Quest applications are distributed through the Meta Horizon Store. The Meta platform itself may collect certain data as part of its operating system and store services, such as app install events and platform telemetry. Our apps are also built with Meta’s software development kit, which may send Meta usage and performance information about that kit. That collection is controlled by Meta, not by ES Designs, and we do not receive it. Please refer to Meta’s Privacy Policy for details on their data practices.

10. Children’s Privacy

Our applications and this website are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Use of Meta Quest is subject to Meta’s own age requirements.

Thrustline and the Anatomy & Physiology Lab collect no personal information from any user, so no child-specific data is at risk. For Vantura, accounts are created by or on behalf of an institution for its enrolled learners. If you believe a child under 13 has created a Vantura account or sent us information through this website, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Data Retention

  • Thrustline: saved data remains on your headset until you use Settings › Reset, uninstall the app, or clear its data through your Meta Quest settings. We hold none of it.
  • Vantura: account, class, enrollment, headset, and viewing records are retained while the account or institutional subscription is active. Uploaded videos are retained until deleted by the institution. On request, we delete an account or an institution’s data; deletion is completed within 30 days. Downloaded video on a headset is removed when the user signs out or the app is uninstalled.
  • Anatomy & Physiology Lab: no data is retained. All session data is cleared when the application closes.
  • This website: form messages are kept for as long as we need them to reply and keep a record of the conversation, and are deleted on request. Email addresses stay on a list until you unsubscribe or ask us to remove them. Google Analytics keeps event-level data for no more than 14 months.

12. Data Security

Thrustline and the Anatomy & Physiology Lab send nothing to our servers, so there is nothing of yours on our side to intercept or breach. What Thrustline saves is kept in the app’s private storage and protected by the Meta Quest platform’s device-level security.

For Vantura and this website, all traffic is encrypted in transit using HTTPS, and stored data is encrypted at rest by our hosting providers. In Vantura, access to class content is enforced at the database level, so an account can only reach videos for classes it teaches or is enrolled in, and video links are short-lived and individually signed rather than publicly accessible. No system is perfectly secure, but we design for least privilege and keep the amount of personal data we hold small.

13. Your Choices and Rights

You may request access to the personal information we hold about you, request a correction, or request deletion. Contact us using the details below and we will respond within 30 days.

  • Thrustline: everything it saves is on your headset and under your control; see “Deleting your data” in Section 2.
  • Vantura: if you use Vantura through your school, your school administers your account, and we may direct requests about student records to your institution. You can stop all data collection by Vantura at any time by signing out of the app, which also removes downloaded lessons from the headset.
  • Email: use the unsubscribe link in any email, or write to us.
  • Analytics: block Google Analytics cookies in your browser or use Google’s opt-out add-on.
