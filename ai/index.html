The detection trap, in four numbers

The case for teaching with AI isn't ideological — it's evidentiary. Detectors don't work, and the market already knows it needs a different answer.

~750 students a 1% false-positive rate wrongly flags per year at one 75,000-paper university — why Vanderbilt disabled its AI detector
61% of TOEFL essays by non-native English writers wrongly flagged as AI (Stanford)
76% of institutions still have no formal AI policy (Tyton Partners)
92% of institutions now have an AI strategy — the will is there (EDUCAUSE 2026)
