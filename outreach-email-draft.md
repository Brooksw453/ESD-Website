---

Hi everyone,

If your inbox looked like mine heading into April, the DOJ Interim Final Rule landing a few days before the original deadline felt like a reprieve. Large public entities now have until **April 26, 2027**. Small entities and special districts have until **April 26, 2028**. That's twelve months of breathing room — not a pass. Complaint-driven enforcement has already started against institutions that missed the April 24 date.

The documents most institutions have been avoiding — course catalogs, faculty handbooks, accreditation self-studies, scanned legacy PDFs — are exactly the ones that take weeks to remediate by hand. If you spend the year well, those get done. If you spend it the way most institutions spend extended deadlines, April 2027 is going to look a lot like April 2026 did.

I'm a faculty member at Quinsigamond Community College. I built **Document Ally** ([accessibility.esdesigns.org](https://accessibility.esdesigns.org)) to fix the smaller documents — syllabi, handouts, short PDFs. It's free, no signup, and it's in active use across MA community colleges. The institutional version, **Document Ally Pro** ([esdesigns.org/#/education/ally-pro](https://esdesigns.org/#/education/ally-pro)), is now in early access, with design-partner pilots available at no cost for a small number of MA institutions.

**A few things worth knowing:**
- Documents are processed in memory and never stored by default — no retention of document contents.
- Output targets WCAG 2.2 AA, a version ahead of the rule's 2.1 AA floor.
- Use access code **MCOAccess** for extended free-tier limits this academic year.
- I'll run the first 10 documents manually for any institution that wants to see the output before committing.
- Credits never expire; institutional plans don't meter per document.
