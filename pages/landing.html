Thrustline · Coming to Meta Quest, December 2026

Race a jet bike powered by your workout.

The harder you work on your machine, the faster and higher you fly.

  • Your effort is the throttle. Work above your hold line to climb; ease off and you sink.
  • Race your ghosts and six rivals. Your best runs ride beside you, and each rival you beat unlocks a faster one.
  • Elliptical, rower, stair climber, or marching in place. The headset reads your rhythm, so there is nothing to pair.

How it's built

From the headset to the server.

The apps, the portals they talk to, and the tools that make them are built in the same studio.

On the headset

Unity and C# for Meta Quest: hand tracking, passthrough, comfort options, and every course profiled on the device until it holds frame rate.

  • Unity 6
  • C#
  • Meta XR SDK
  • OpenXR

Behind it

Vantura's instructor portal, accounts, class rosters, and video delivery: front end, API, and database, built and run here.

  • Next.js
  • TypeScript
  • Supabase
  • Postgres
  • Cloudflare R2

The tools that make them

Thrustline's courses come from a level generator written for it: roads, terrain, zones, and pickups in one pass, so a new course starts as a design decision.

  • Procedural generation
  • Python
  • Claude Code
