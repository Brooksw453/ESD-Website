How it plays

Your effort is the throttle.

  1. Calibrate

    Set up a profile for your machine once. A short calibration learns your cruising pace, so effort is measured against you, not a stranger.

  2. Ride

    Push above the hold line to take off and climb. Fly through launch gates, catch boosters and speed chevrons, and find the door in each laser wall.

  3. Race

    Three laps against the ghosts of your best runs, or against a rival. Each race saves your lap times, effort, distance, and estimated calories.

Works with

The machine you already have.

No sensor, no cable, no app to pair. The headset reads the rhythm of your movement.

  • Elliptical Stride as you normally would.
  • Rowing machine Seated, and full body.
  • Stair climber Every step counts as effort.
  • Marching in place No machine needed.

Courses

Four zones a lap, so you learn the line.

Every course is split into four areas with their own landmarks, so by the second lap you know where the next climb is. More courses arrive before launch.

  • City Circuit

    Downtown towers, the harbour cranes, Central Park, and Old Town.

  • Town Hills

    Main Street, Church Hill, Lakeside Park, and Orchard Lane. The climbs slow you down.

  • Alpine Ridge

    A valley lake, a pine climb, a snowy summit ridge, and a canyon under a stone arch.

  • Tropic Isles

    Lighthouse Point, the Palm Keys, a smoking volcano, and the sea arches.

  • Night City

    The Neon Strip, the Night Market, Runway Lights, and Skyline Heights. Every building lit.

  • Lunar Run

    Base Camp, the Crater Fields, the Highlands, and Shadow Canyon, with Earth overhead.

Hangar

Earn it, ride it.

Coins from your races buy new bikes and paint finishes, from metallic to chrome to neon. Bikes handle differently, but none is simply faster, so your records keep measuring your effort.

Comfort and access

Hands only, if you like.

Thrustline is fully playable with hand tracking, start to finish, with no controllers to hold while you work out. A comfort vignette softens the view in flight, music can be turned down or off, and passthrough shows your room while you set up.

Questions

Before you ride.

Which headsets does it run on?

Thrustline is built for Meta Quest 3 and Quest 3S. Quest 2 and Quest Pro are being tested before launch.

Do I need a sensor or a connected machine?

No. The headset reads the rhythm of your movement. Any elliptical, rowing machine, or stair climber works, or no machine at all if you march in place.

How much space do I need?

Your machine, set up the way you normally use it, with a clear area around it. Thrustline shows your room through passthrough while you set up.

Are the calories accurate?

They are an estimate, based on your effort against your own calibrated pace. Thrustline is a game, not a medical or fitness device.

What does it collect about me?

Nothing leaves your headset. There is no account, no advertising, and no analytics. The details are in the privacy policy.

When and where can I get it?

December 2026 on the Meta Horizon Store, as a one-time purchase with no subscription. Join the launch list and you'll get one email the day it's out.
