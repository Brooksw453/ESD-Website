About

From the inside.

I spent 21 years inside higher education before going full time on ES Designs in May 2026. Most of that time I led online learning, instructional design, and faculty development — first as a senior faculty member and Director of the Center for Excellence in Learning & Teaching at Cambridge College, then as Executive Director of Online Learning & the Center for Academic Excellence at Quinsigamond Community College. I led a college-wide emergency pivot to remote learning during COVID, and watched accessibility offices drown in document backlogs they had no tools to solve.

Somewhere along the way I grew frustrated with the pace of institutional innovation. The vendors were too expensive, the tools didn’t match real workflows, and the people doing the actual work — faculty, disability services staff, instructional designers — were left to figure it out with PDFs and good intentions. So I taught myself to code. C#, Python, JavaScript. I founded ES Designs in 2022 to build the things I wished had existed when I was on the other side of the desk.

ES Designs is now the integrated Title II compliance planning system for higher education. Five products, one path: a free 9-chapter Compliance Roadmap Tool, an AI Website Audit, role-matched WCAG 2.2 training, Document Ally Pro for batch document remediation, and continuous monitoring. The plan is free because the planning is the part nobody can do for you — once you’ve planned, you’ll know what to buy and from whom.

I hold an MFA in Creative Writing (Poetry) from Lesley University, an MA in English Writing and Teaching from UMass Boston, and a BA from UMass Lowell. I’m based in Massachusetts. ES Designs is built by an educator for educators — not by an enterprise IT vendor repurposing their compliance product line.
