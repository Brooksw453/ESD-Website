About

Classroom to codebase.

I spent 17 years inside higher education — faculty, instructional designer, Director of a Center for Teaching and Learning, and eventually Executive Director of Online Learning. I led a college-wide emergency pivot to remote learning during COVID, and spent years watching accessibility offices drown in document backlogs they had no tools to solve.

Somewhere along the way, I grew frustrated with the pace of institutional innovation. The vendors were too expensive, the tools didn't match real workflows, and the people doing the actual work — faculty, disability services staff, instructional designers — were left to figure it out with PDFs and good intentions. So I taught myself to code. C#, Python, JavaScript. I shipped VR learning apps to Meta Quest, built AI tools on Azure, and in 2022 I founded ES Designs to build the things I wished had existed when I was on the other side of the desk.

In May 2026, I left my state position to run ES Designs full time. Document Ally came out of the same frustration that drove every role I've held — I watched accessibility coordinators spend weeks remediating a single course catalog by hand. Document Ally Pro is the institutional version, purpose-built for the Title II era, priced for community college budgets, and designed by someone who actually sat in those compliance meetings.

I hold an MFA in Creative Writing from Lesley University, an MA in English Writing and Teaching from UMass Boston, and a BA from UMass Lowell. I'm based in Massachusetts. ES Designs is my full-time focus — building the accessibility and learning tools higher ed actually needs.
