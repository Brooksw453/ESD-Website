About

Education Simulation Designs

ES Designs builds immersive experiences that inspire movement, learning, and engagement. From VR fitness adventures on Meta Quest to AI-powered education technology for higher education — we craft tools that connect people to new possibilities.

Founded by Brooks Winchell and based in Massachusetts, ES Designs has been pushing the boundaries of spatial computing and AI since 2022.

With a background in higher education administration and a passion for open educational resources, Brooks built ES Designs to solve real problems he saw firsthand — inaccessible course materials, static textbooks, and the gap between emerging technology and classroom practice.
