Why we're building Pro

The free Document Ally solves accessibility for individual documents. Pro solves it for institutions.

⏱️

The deadline is here

Title II is now live for large public institutions. Small entities have until April 2027. Most are nowhere close on documents, and enforcement actions have already begun.

📚

Documents are the hard part

Course catalogs. Syllabi. Faculty handbooks. Board reports. Scanned legacy PDFs. The document layer is where compliance breaks down — and where enterprise vendor tools are weakest.

🎓

Built by an educator

Siteimprove and Level Access are built for Fortune 500 IT teams. Overlay vendors get sued. Document Ally Pro is built by someone who worked inside higher ed for the people actually doing the remediation.

What Pro will include at launch

Shaped by conversations with accessibility offices like yours.

✓ Shipping at launch

  • Account-based access with Supabase auth
