Walk through the human body.

A self-directed VR anatomy lab for students who can’t get into a physical one.

The Anatomy & Physiology Lab is an immersive learning adventure for Meta Quest. Students explore skeletal, cranial, and cardiovascular systems with interactive 3D models, then check their understanding with built-in learning modules and knowledge-check quizzes. Built for nursing, allied-health, and intro-biology programs where cadaver or model-lab access is limited — by an educator who spent 21 years inside higher ed.

Inside the lab

Three interactive environments, each focused on a system students struggle to visualize from a textbook.

Skull & Skeletal Lab

Explore the human skeleton and cranial bones with interactive 3D models and detailed info panels. Rotate, isolate, and label structures at the student’s own pace.

Heart Lab

Study lifelike heart models, watch the rhythmic beat, and learn cardiovascular anatomy through immersive, hands-on interaction rather than static diagrams.

VR Playground

A low-stakes space for hand-tracking demos and exploration — a comfortable on-ramp for students new to VR before they enter the structured labs.

Built for learning, not just looking

Learning modules & knowledge checks

Exploration is the hook; the modules are how it actually teaches.

Each environment pairs free exploration with structured learning modules — slide-based instruction followed by knowledge-check quizzes so students can confirm they understood what they just explored. It’s self-directed: a student can run a full session without an instructor present, which is the point for programs with limited lab time.
