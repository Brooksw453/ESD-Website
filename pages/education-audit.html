What it checks

Automated WCAG 2.2 Level AA rules, run in a real browser and grouped by principle. Software cannot judge everything: keyboard flow, focus order, dragging alternatives (2.5.7), and whether alt text is any good still need a person.

Perceivable

  • Color contrast (1.4.3)
  • Alt text and text alternatives (1.1.1)
  • Headings, lists, and table structure (1.3.1)
  • Zoom and scaling not disabled (1.4.4)

Operable

  • Skip links and bypass blocks (2.4.1)
  • Page titles and link purpose (2.4.2, 2.4.4)
  • Scrollable regions reachable by keyboard (2.1.1)
  • Target size, new in 2.2 (2.5.8)

Understandable

  • Language of page (3.1.1)
  • Language of parts (3.1.2)
  • Form fields with labels (3.3.2)

Robust

  • Valid name, role, value (4.1.2)
  • Buttons, links, and frames with accessible names
  • Valid ARIA roles and attributes

Why the fix matters

Most audit tools give you a spreadsheet of failures. This one gives your developer ticket-ready fixes.
