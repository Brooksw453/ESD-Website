What we check

Full WCAG 2.2 Level AA coverage, with extra focus on the issues that most often land public institutions in OCR complaints.

Perceivable

  • Color contrast (1.4.3)
  • Alt text and text alternatives (1.1.1)
  • Captions and audio descriptions (1.2.x)
  • Resize and reflow (1.4.4, 1.4.10)

Operable

  • Keyboard accessibility (2.1.x)
  • Focus visibility and target size (2.4.7, 2.5.8)
  • Skip links and navigation (2.4.1, 2.4.3)
  • Dragging alternatives — new in 2.2 (2.5.7)

Understandable

  • Language of page & parts (3.1.x)
  • Consistent navigation and identification (3.2.x)
  • Form labels and instructions (3.3.x)
  • Redundant entry — new in 2.2 (3.3.7)

Robust

  • Valid name, role, value (4.1.2)
  • Status messages (4.1.3)
  • ARIA usage audit
  • Parsing and markup quality
