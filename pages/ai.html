The detection trap, in four numbers

The case for teaching with AI isn't ideological. It's evidentiary. Detectors aren't reliable enough to base an accusation on, and institutions are already looking for a different answer.

~750 papers a 1% false-positive rate would wrongly flag in a year of 75,000 submissions. That math is why Vanderbilt turned its AI detector off.
61% of TOEFL essays by non-native English writers wrongly flagged as AI (Stanford)
76% of institutions still have no formal AI policy (Tyton Partners)
92% of institutions now have an AI strategy, so the will is there (EDUCAUSE 2026)
