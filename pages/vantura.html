Questions programs ask

Is this an LMS?

No. Vantura is the immersive piece that sits alongside Canvas or Moodle, not a replacement for them. Gradebook passback is on the roadmap for programs that want it.

Do we need special headsets or an IT project?

No. Vantura runs on the consumer Quest 2, Quest 3, Quest 3S, and Quest Pro headsets you already own. Install the free app from the Meta Horizon Store and pair each headset once with a code. Nothing is sideloaded and nothing needs a device-management system.

What if our classroom Wi-Fi is unreliable?

That is the case Vantura is built for. Videos download ahead of class and play locally.

Who owns the content?

You do. Vantura hosts and delivers what your faculty create. It is your library.

Can we try it before we buy?

Yes. The free plan covers one instructor and one class, and it comes with sample 360 lessons already in the library. Start there, then add a pilot when you are ready.

What video formats work?

MP4 in H.265, up to 8K. The Quest hardware decodes H.264 only up to 4K, so above that the portal asks for H.265 and tells you exactly which export setting or command fixes the file. Most 360 cameras record H.265 already; it is usually the phone app’s export that switches it.

How do faculty sign in?

With Google or Microsoft, or with an email address and password. If your campus Microsoft tenant blocks new apps until IT approves them, use email and password. Password resets arrive as a one-time code rather than a link, so campus mail security cannot spend the reset before you open it.

How does billing work?

Classroom is a card subscription on the portal, monthly or annual. Annual plans renew automatically with a reminder email three days before, and you can cancel from the billing page at any time, effective at the end of the period. Department and Campus are quoted and invoiced, with purchase orders welcome.
