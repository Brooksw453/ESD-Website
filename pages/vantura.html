Immersive video for higher ed and CTE

Your environments, on your headsets, for your whole class.

Vantura hosts the 360 videos your own faculty film, your labs, clinics, shops, and field sites, and delivers them to the Quest headsets your school already owns. No per-student fees. No enterprise device stack. It plays even when the Wi-Fi does not.

VR in the classroom usually means someone else's content.

Programs that want immersive learning face a hard choice. License a canned library of scenarios at roughly 100 dollars per student that you cannot change, or try to sideload your own videos onto headsets and hope the file plays and the network holds. Neither fits how faculty actually teach, with their own spaces and their own students.

Vantura is the third option: your content, your headsets, handled for you.

Film your real spaces.

Faculty record their own labs, clinics, and field sites in 360 and upload through a simple web portal. No production team required.

Headsets pair with a code.

Open the free Vantura app on a Quest, pair it once with a short code from the class portal, and the lesson library downloads. Nothing to sideload, no device-management system to buy.

Class just runs.

Videos play from the headset, so seeking is instant and a weak connection never stops the lesson. Set the headset down, pick it back up, keep going.

From your camera to the headset in four steps

Step 1

Record.

Faculty film in 360 with a consumer camera. A short guide covers the settings that look best on a headset.

Step 2

Upload.

Drop the video into the Vantura web portal. It checks the format and organizes it by class.

Step 3

Pair the headsets.

Each headset pairs once with a short code at the class portal. A lab tech can walk a whole cart in one pass.

Step 4

Teach.

Videos download to each headset and are ready for class, online or off.

Questions programs ask

Is this an LMS?

No. Vantura is the immersive piece that sits alongside Canvas or Moodle, not a replacement for them. Gradebook passback is on the roadmap for programs that want it.

Do we need special headsets or an IT project?

No. Vantura runs on the consumer Quest 3 and 3S headsets you already own. Install the free app and pair each headset once with a code.

What if our classroom Wi-Fi is unreliable?

That is the case Vantura is built for. Videos download ahead of class and play locally.

Who owns the content?

You do. Vantura hosts and delivers what your faculty create. It is your library.

Can we try it before we buy?

Yes. The free plan covers one instructor and one class. Start there, then add a pilot when you are ready.
