# Syllabus A11Y — Campaign Plan
**"Use the Year"**
*April 2026 – April 2027 | 12-Month Higher-Ed Accessibility Outreach*

---

> **Reframe note (April 2026):** This plan replaces the original "Accessible by April 24" 5-week sprint. The DOJ Interim Final Rule (effective April 20, 2026) extended ADA Title II web-compliance dates by one year — large entities now April 26, 2027; small entities and special districts now April 26, 2028. The deadline-panic opening is gone. The opportunity shifted from "help them not miss a deadline" to "help them spend the window on the hard documents instead of the easy ones." Same tool, same audiences, a warmer opener and a longer runway.

---

## 1. Campaign Overview

**Campaign Name:** Use the Year

**One-sentence summary:** A 12-month higher-ed outreach campaign that converts the DOJ Title II extension into adoption of Document Ally (free) and Document Ally Pro (institutional) across MA community colleges, the MCO network, AHEAD, and regional consortia.

**Primary Objective:** Get 8–15 MA community colleges and regional universities actively using Document Ally and/or piloting Document Ally Pro by April 2027, with 3–5 converting to paid institutional licenses.

**Secondary Objectives:**
- Establish Brooks as the recognized voice on AI-assisted document accessibility in higher ed
- Collect 3–5 testimonials or documented before/after outcomes
- Build a pipeline of 30+ qualified institutional contacts for post-extension follow-up
- Land 1–2 conference talks (AHEAD, Accessing Higher Ground, EDUCAUSE) for the 2026–2027 cycle

## 2. Target Audiences

### Track A — Internal (QCC Team) | *Months 1–2*
- **Who:** Your QCC colleagues — faculty, instructional designers, disability services staff, department chair, academic technology staff.
- **New pain point:** The April 2026 panic is gone. What remains is a year to do the thing right — catalog, faculty handbooks, legacy PDFs. That's Ally Pro's sweet spot, not Ally's.
- **Motivation:** A colleague-built tool that handles the *hard* documents, not just the easy ones.
- **Buying stage:** Testers and champions. Their adoption anchors the QCC case study.

### Track B — MCO Listserv Network | *Months 2–4*
- **Who:** Instructional designers, faculty development staff, disability/ADA coordinators, academic technology directors at MA community colleges.
- **New pain point:** Many already triaged syllabi before April 2026. The remaining backlog is the 200–500 page documents everyone has been avoiding.
- **Motivation:** Free tool to try now, paid institutional option when budget cycles allow.
- **Buying stage:** Awareness → trial → institutional conversation. Extended timeline means a second touchpoint in the fall budget cycle.
- **Key insight:** "You just got a year — here's what we'd do with it" is a warmer opener than "the deadline is Tuesday."

### Track C — Targeted 1:1 Outreach | *Months 2–8*
- **Who:** 10–15 specific stakeholders at MA colleges + 10–15 regional peers you know.
- **Motivation:** Personal recommendation from a trusted peer; longer runway to get on their roadmap.

### Track D — AHEAD & Regional Consortia | *Months 3–9*
- **Who:** ADA/504 coordinators, disability services directors nationally via AHEAD; NY/CT/CA/TX community-college consortia.
- **Motivation:** Extended deadlines opened a window for national outreach that wasn't worth it in a 5-week sprint.

## 3. Key Messages

**Core message:** The DOJ gave you a year. Use it to fix the documents that were going to take you a year anyway.

**Supporting messages:**

1. *"This is a window, not a pass."*
   — Complaint-driven enforcement has already started against entities that missed April 2026. The extension is breathing room for remediation work, not permission to defer it.

2. *"Document Ally handles what you already had handled. Document Ally Pro handles what you've been avoiding."*
   — Syllabi and small PDFs are the easy part. Course catalogs, faculty handbooks, accreditation self-studies, scanned legacy PDFs — those are the ones that take weeks by hand. That's what Ally Pro is built for.

3. *"Built by a community-college educator, not an enterprise vendor."*
   — Siteimprove and Level Access are priced for Fortune 500 IT. Overlay vendors get sued. Document Ally is built by someone who sat in the compliance meetings.

4. *"Privacy-first and FERPA-aware."*
   — Documents processed in-browser; only the remediation pass touches an AI provider under a zero-retention agreement. No stored documents by default.

### Channel 3: Personal 1:1 Outreach
*Months 2–8 | Effort: Medium | Cost: $0*
Email or LinkedIn message to 20+ specific contacts. Reference a specific shared challenge ("I know your team had a lot on their plate heading into April — the extension is a gift if you spend it right").

### Channel 4: LinkedIn Thought Leadership
*Ongoing | Effort: Medium | Cost: $0*
Biweekly posts on the accessibility side of your LinkedIn: WCAG 2.2 explainers, extension commentary, before/after remediation case studies, enforcement-action analysis. Goal: 2–3 posts picked up by AHEAD / accessibility Slack communities.

### Channel 5: AHEAD Community
*Months 3–9 | Effort: Medium | Cost: $0*
AHEAD regional chapters + online community. Propose a session for the next AHEAD cycle: *"AI-Assisted Document Remediation: What to Do With the Extra Year."*

### Channel 6: Conference Circuit
*Months 4–12 | Effort: High | Cost: Travel*
- AHEAD (primary)
- Accessing Higher Ground (technical accessibility)
- EDUCAUSE (IT-accessibility)
- CSUN Assistive Technology Conference (industry)

### Channel 7: Quarterly Webinar
*Months 4, 7, 10 | Effort: Medium | Cost: $0*
*"What we found auditing [N] community colleges this quarter."* Uses anonymized data from the free audit outreach. Every registrant is a qualified lead.

### Channel 8: Channel Partners
*Months 6–12*
Regional accessibility consultants and small ed-tech advisory firms. 20% revenue share on institutional deals they bring in.

## 9. Risks and Mitigations

**Risk 1: Extension dissolves urgency entirely.**
Mitigation: Lead messaging with enforcement actions already underway. The deadline changed; the exposure didn't.

**Risk 2: Paid conversion lags because budget cycles slipped.**
Mitigation: Plan on two budget-cycle windows (fall 2026 and spring 2027 for FY27 budgets) rather than one.

**Risk 3: Free-tool adoption without paid conversion.**
Mitigation: Lead with Ally for easy documents, but every 1:1 conversation names the large-document problem Ally Pro solves. Document Ally is the door; Document Ally Pro is the room.

**Risk 4: Outreach fatigue on the MCO listserv.**
Mitigation: One listserv post per academic year. All follow-up goes 1:1 off-list.
