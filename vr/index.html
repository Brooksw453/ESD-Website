Thrustline

A jet bike race powered by your workout. The headset reads the rhythm of your stride or stroke on an elliptical, rower, or stair climber, and your effort becomes thrust. Race your ghosts and six rivals across six courses, playable with hands only.

  • Unity 6
  • Hand tracking
  • Procedural courses
  • Profiled on the headset

Vantura

Every other VR option hands a program someone else’s scenarios. Vantura hosts the real places a program teaches in, filmed by its own faculty. Faculty upload 360 video through a web portal, each headset pairs once with a short code, and the lesson library downloads to the device, so a weak classroom network never stops a lesson.

  • Unity
  • Next.js portal
  • Supabase
  • Cloudflare R2
  • Offline playback

Anatomy & Physiology Lab

An interactive human-anatomy lab for students who can’t get into a physical one. Students walk through the skeletal, cranial, and cardiovascular systems with interactive 3D models, then check their understanding with built-in knowledge checks. Built for nursing, allied-health, and intro-biology programs.

Thrustline and the A&P Lab need no account and send nothing to us. Vantura is an account-based service a school runs for its own classes, so it stores what a class needs and nothing more. Each is set out in full in the privacy policy.
