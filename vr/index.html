Vantura

Your environments, on your headsets, for your whole class.

Every other VR option hands a program someone else’s scenarios. Vantura hosts the real places your program teaches in, filmed by your own faculty. Faculty upload 360 video through a web portal, each headset pairs once with a short code, and the lesson library downloads to the device.

Because playback is local, seeking is instant and a weak classroom network never stops a lesson. There is nothing to sideload and no device-management system to buy, and it is priced by the department rather than per student.

Anatomy & Physiology Lab

An interactive human-anatomy lab for students who can’t get into a physical one.

A self-directed VR learning adventure exploring human anatomy and physiology. Students walk through skeletal, cranial, and cardiovascular systems with interactive 3D models, then check their understanding with built-in learning modules and knowledge-check quizzes. Built for nursing, allied-health, and intro-biology programs where lab access is limited.
